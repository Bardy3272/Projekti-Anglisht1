Questions

Good to know

Can a family member arrange support for me?

Yes. A family member or caregiver can submit a request on your behalf.

What if I don’t use the internet?

People who don’t use the internet can contact us by phone.

Are medicine reminders medical advice?

No. Reminders follow the schedule you or your caregiver gives us and never replace advice from a doctor or pharmacist.

Who makes the friendly visits?

Trained helpers or volunteers who come for a chat, a walk or a little company.

Who sees my family updates?

Only family members you give permission to.
